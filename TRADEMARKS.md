# Names and logos

The GPL covers this code, not our names. “Oases”, “Oases Chat”, “Ogent”,
“QingTeng”, “青藤” and the twin-pools logo are QingTeng Studio's marks.

You may say that your work is based on Oases Chat. If you distribute a
modified version, give it a different name and icon, and don't present it
as an official Oases Chat build. The logos of third-party services in
`OasesChat/Resources/Assets.xcassets/ProviderLogos/` belong to those
services' owners and only identify each service.
